Conclusion

Taxation directly shapes the financial performance and competitiveness of small tourism enterprises.

1

Choose the tax regime by profitability, eligibility and total burden, not by the nominal rate alone.

2

Raising VAT from 12% to 16% can lift room prices and may reduce tourist demand.

3

Tourism contributions support sustainable development when revenue is allocated transparently.

“Tourism taxation should not simply raise revenue. It should balance business competitiveness, affordable travel and sustainable destinations.”
